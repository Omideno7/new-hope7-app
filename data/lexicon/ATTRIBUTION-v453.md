# Original-language study assets
Recovered core glossary: Omideno7 project v1 (160 entries) and lexical fields from Omideno7_Biblical_Word_Bank_Phase2_200_Trilingual.json (200 entries, 120 additional Strong keys). Duplicate keys were combined. Old placeholder prose and unverified verse associations are not published.

Canonical headwords/transliteration: Open Scriptures Strong's Dictionaries, commit 0acd2f251c2d35ff8db2dece4e0593979d3ac223; public-domain dictionary material.

Greek selected-reference verification: Tauber, J. K., ed. (2017), MorphGNT: SBLGNT Edition 6.12, commit aaed91e57c8e4a8dc9a2383e129ca5e75fe6393d, https://github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt . Source morphological analysis and lemmatization: CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The adapted selected-reference mappings here are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). No complete Greek verse text is redistributed here.

Hebrew/Aramaic verification: Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible Project, commit 3d15126fb1ef74867fc1434be1942e837932691f, https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb . Source Hebrew morphological data: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The combined adapted selected-reference mapping fields are CC BY-SA 4.0; this license does not cover the independently authored app code. WLC verse mapping is respected; ambiguous partial mappings were excluded.

This resource is not a complete original-language Bible or a word-for-word interlinear. Only selected verified associations are shown. A translation label is not proof of one original lemma, and related terms are not automatically interchangeable. The app's Bible/Apocrypha corpus remains unchanged.

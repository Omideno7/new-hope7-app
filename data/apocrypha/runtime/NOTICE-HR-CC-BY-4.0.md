# Croatian prepared-text attribution

The prepared Croatian text used for Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch,
Letter of Jeremiah, Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, First Maccabees, and Second
Maccabees was extracted from:

- **Croatian Bible Translation (Kršćanska sadašnjost, 1987)**
- Digital edition edited by **Neven Jovanović**, University of Zagreb
- Source: <https://github.com/nevenjovanovic/biblija-hr>
- Source commit: `b36295606eb4b073d5172a1ac529c9843dfb8dcb`
- Source file: `zefaniaxml/2017-06-03_HRV_BIBLIJAKS_(BIBLIJA PRIJEVOD KS).xml`
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**
  (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>)

Modification notice: the source wording is unchanged. The data was extracted
from Zefania XML and regrouped into the New Hope 7 nineteen-book Apocrypha
scope. Baruch chapter 6 is presented as the Letter of Jeremiah, while Daniel
chapters 13 and 14 are presented as Susanna and Bel and the Dragon.

This attribution does not apply to the newly translated Croatian review rows,
which are separately marked `in_review` in their source packages.
